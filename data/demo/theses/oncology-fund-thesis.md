# Granite Ridge Bio Ventures: Fund II Investment Thesis

*Synthetic sample thesis for demonstrating the VC Due Diligence Tool. The fund and its people are fictional.*

## Who we are

Granite Ridge Bio Ventures is an early-stage life-sciences fund. Fund II is 120 million dollars, and we lead seed and Series A rounds of 3 to 10 million dollars in new therapeutics companies, often built around university licenses.

## What we invest in

- **Immuno-oncology therapeutics.** Cell therapies, antibodies, oncolytic viruses and other medicines that help the immune system attack cancer.
- **Solid tumors.** We look for approaches that overcome the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment, where today's checkpoint inhibitors and CAR T cells fail.
- **Platforms over single assets** when the platform can produce several drug candidates.

## Problems we want solved

1. Most patients do not respond to PD-1 and PD-L1 checkpoint inhibitors.
2. CAR T cells work in blood cancers but not yet in solid tumors.
3. Effective immune-stimulating drugs, such as IL-12 and STING agonists, are too toxic when given to the whole body.

## Stage

We invest from late preclinical through Phase 1. We want efficacy in at least one animal model and a credible plan to file an IND within about two years. We do not fund early discovery without animal data.

## Deal structure

We need an **exclusive license** to issued patents or published patent applications, so the company we build owns its core IP. We will form a new company around the license or co-invest with an existing startup. We do not take non-exclusive licenses.

## Outside our scope

Diagnostics and laboratory tests, medical devices, digital health, autoimmune and inflammatory diseases, animal health, and anything outside therapeutics.
