# Northwind Climate Hardware Fund: Investment Thesis

*Synthetic sample thesis for demonstrating the VC Due Diligence Tool. The fund and its people are fictional.*

## Who we are

Northwind is a climate technology fund that backs hardware companies decarbonizing energy and industry. We write first checks of 1 to 5 million dollars at seed and Series A.

## What we invest in

- **Energy storage.** Batteries, battery materials and long-duration storage that reduce cost or remove scarce minerals such as lithium, cobalt and platinum.
- **Clean energy materials and devices.** Solar, hydrogen and electrification hardware that can be made with existing factories.
- **Emissions sensing.** Sensors and monitoring hardware that find methane and other greenhouse gas emissions.

## Problems we want solved

1. Grid and industrial storage is too expensive, and lithium and cobalt supply is constrained.
2. Industrial heat and hydrogen production still burn fossil fuels.
3. Methane leaks go undetected for weeks, and new regulations require continuous monitoring.

## Stage

We back working lab prototypes through field pilots. We want measured performance data from a prototype; pure concept work is too early. We also avoid capital-heavy first-of-a-kind plants.

## Deal structure

We prefer an exclusive license or a spinout company that owns patents or patent applications. A field-of-use license can work for materials. We do not invest in software-only companies.

## Outside our scope

Software and data products, consumer goods, healthcare and life sciences, agriculture, and buildings-only products.
